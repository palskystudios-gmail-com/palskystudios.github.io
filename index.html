01 · The night

Before there were screens, there was the sky.

On every continent, for as long as people have gathered, they have looked up, reading stories in the stars and naming them after heroes, harvests and home.

PALSKY brings back that ritual. Our drones are the stars, and the night is the stage for the stories that make a place what it is, wherever that place may be.

02 · The people

Akwaaba. Karibu. Bienvenue. You are welcome here.

From Accra to Rio, Lagos to London, Nairobi to Tokyo, every community has its own way of greeting, celebrating and remembering. We start by listening to the people behind the moment: their festivals, their language, their symbols.

That way the sky speaks in their voice, not ours.

03 · Heritage

The future is built from what we carry forward.

Adinkra symbols from Ghana, Celtic knots, Japanese family crests, the patterns woven into kente and tartan: every culture keeps its wisdom in a visual language. We lift it above the city so a new generation can look up and see where they come from.

“Se wo were fi na wosankofa a yenkyi.”It is not wrong to go back for what you forgot. The Sankofa proverb.

04 · Organizations

Your culture, not just your logo.

From start-ups to global brands, every organization has a culture: the values it lives by, the milestones it fought for, the people who built it.

We choreograph launches, anniversaries and campaigns scene by scene, so your audience feels where you came from and where you are going.

05 · Industries

From the farm to the world’s skylines.

Industries shape the culture of nations: the farmers, fishermen, makers and builders. A drone show can honour the hands behind the work.

Picture a cocoa pod from Ghana opening into a gift, a wind turbine turning over the sea, a ship crossing the night, or a skyline rising from the ground. The story of an industry, told to the people it serves.
